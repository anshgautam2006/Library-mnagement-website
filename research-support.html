Research Support

Practical guidance for finding, evaluating and using information.

The library supports students and researchers at every stage of their work, from refining a question to locating sources and managing citations.

Finding literature

Search the library catalogue for books and journals, then explore the e-resources for databases and full-text research.

Research tools

Ask the library about citation management, subject databases, institutional research and access to licensed resources.

Get assistance

Contact the library with your topic, course or research question so staff can point you to relevant sources.
