User Services

Library services for students, researchers and faculty.

The library team can help you use the catalogue, access online resources and make effective use of research tools. Check with the library for current eligibility and service procedures.

Borrowing and renewals

Use your library account to review loans, due dates and renewal options. Items with a hold or overdue status may have different renewal rules.

Library orientation

New to the library? Ask about an orientation session covering the catalogue, collections, study spaces and digital resources.

Research help

Get support with information discovery, citation tools and research databases through Research Support.
