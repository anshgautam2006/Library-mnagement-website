Rules & Guidelines

A shared set of guidelines helps keep the library welcoming and useful.

Please follow library instructions and respect the study needs of others. Specific borrowing limits, fines and access conditions are set by the university and may be updated by the library.

Using the space

Keep noise low in reading areas, set mobile devices to silent and leave study spaces tidy for the next visitor. Food and drink should be limited to designated areas.

Using collections

Handle books and equipment with care. Return borrowed items by their due date and ask staff before removing reference materials from the library.

Digital access

Use university accounts responsibly. Follow publisher licence terms and do not share passwords or download content in ways that disrupt access for others.
