About the Library

A welcoming place for learning, research and discovery.

The Central Library of Babasaheb Bhimrao Ambedkar University supports teaching, independent learning and research across disciplines. Our collections bring together print books, journals, reference materials and digital resources for the university community.

Our mission

To make knowledge easy to discover and use, support responsible scholarship, and provide an inclusive environment for every learner.

Library facilities

Reading spaces, reference assistance, discovery tools, digital access and research guidance are available to help students and faculty make the most of the collection.
